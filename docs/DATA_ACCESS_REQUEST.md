# 可选扩展：原始纯手部数据申请

2026-10-29：本轮已补33张作者明确标为Asian的真实场景原图，覆盖多人、复杂背景、手脸干扰和偏色/不均匀光照。两张纯手部测试图来自真实场景裁剪，不冒充独立拍摄的原始场景。

若需要额外的独立原始纯手部样本，[SHAPE 作者页](https://users.soict.hust.edu.vn/linhdt/dataset/)要求邮件联系 linhdt@soict.hust.edu.vn，[作者论文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00521-022-07651-1)说明亚洲参与者。本项目未发送邮件、未取得完整SHAPE数据。以下为可审阅草稿，不作为本轮已交付内容：

> Subject: Research access request for SHAPE RGB images and annotations
>
> Dear Dataset Authors,
>
> I am [name] from [institution]. Our educational research evaluates traditional YCbCr skin-color and face-region segmentation, including false detections on hands. Could you grant research/educational access to original RGB hand-only and hand-face images, segmentation annotations and author-provided Asian population labels? Anonymous subject IDs, if available, would help group samples for evaluation. Please advise on permitted local processing, example figures, derivative mask sharing, citations and redistribution restrictions. We will not redistribute original images without permission.
>
> Thank you, [name / institution / contact]

取得后检查作者标签、原图与标注、可获得的人物/场景分组及许可；没有人物编号不直接拒收，须说明身份重叠无法排除。新数据另作外部测试，不回流已报告模型的阈值选择。该申请不是本轮 A 交付的必需步骤，现有交付的范围与数值以 [A_STATUS.md](A_STATUS.md)为准。

# 剩余场景的数据申请材料

已有 220 张原始照片满足规模与人物数量目标。真实亚洲多人同框、复杂正样本背景、强偏色光照和完整手部干扰原始场景仍缺；只接受作者明确人群标签，不从外貌推断。

[SHAPE 作者页](https://users.soict.hust.edu.vn/linhdt/dataset/)要求邮件联系 linhdt@soict.hust.edu.vn；[作者论文](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00521-022-07651-1)说明亚洲参与者。网页说明图不能代替原始场景。本项目未发送邮件、未取得完整数据。

申请至少 40 张原始 RGB 手部场景，需作者提供匿名人物 ID、人群标签、原始分割标注、室内/室外信息和研究/教学使用许可；尽可能包含脸手同框及纯手部图。有脸需另制 face 参考。SHAPE 未被证明覆盖多人或强色光，不能声称这些场景已补齐。

以下是可审阅的申请草稿，尚未发送；姓名、机构由申请人填写。

> Subject: Research access request for SHAPE RGB images and annotations
>
> Dear Dataset Authors,
>
> I am [name] from [institution]. Our educational research project evaluates a traditional YCbCr skin-color model and face-region segmentation, including false detections on hands. We found your SHAPE dataset and accompanying paper.
>
> Could you grant research/educational access to original RGB images and segmentation annotations, together with anonymous participant IDs and author-provided population labels? We require explicitly documented Asian participants and will not infer ethnicity from appearance. A subset covering at least 40 original indoor/outdoor hand scenes, including hands with faces where available and hand-only scenes, would be helpful.
>
> Please advise on permitted local processing, example figures in academic reports, derivative mask sharing, citations and redistribution restrictions. We will retain subject-disjoint splits and not redistribute original images without permission.
>
> Thank you,
> [name / institution / contact]

取得后校验作者与使用条件、原图标注对应关系、人物重叠，划为独立外部压力测试，不回流已有训练/阈值选择。多人和特殊光照仍需另找明确标签和许可的原始来源，目前没有可交付下载包。
